How Strigoi finds its makers

A bat does not search the night by sight. It calls into the dark and reads the shape of whatever the dark sends back. Strigoi finds its makers the same way: it sends its signal out and listens for the work that answers, loam heavy in the seam, a wing pulled thinner than it should hold, an angle wrong in the right way. When that pull is heard in someone's hands, the founder goes to them. Today, every maker in the circle is there by her invitation. There is no application form.

If you hear the signal

If the signal reached you, and something in your work answered before you did, write to the address below. Let the work speak first. [What a maker might send.]

What happens then

Nothing is filed, and nothing waits in a queue. The founder reads what reaches her, and she answers, or she doesn't. What she bites, Strigoi immortalizes. [When to expect a reply, if at all.]
