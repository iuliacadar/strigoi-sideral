How to measure yourself, and how the sizes compare. Measure your body, not a piece of clothing you own.

How to measure

  • Chest: around the fullest part, under the arms.
  • Waist: around the narrowest part.
  • Hips: around the fullest part.

Use a soft tape measure over light clothing, and keep it level, snug but not tight. It is easier if someone else holds the tape. [Any further measuring advice.]
