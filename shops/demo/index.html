Vârcolac Atelier

One workroom and the hands in it. It makes grave-cloth: things to be worn close and worn down. Wool is cut raw and left to fray. Linen is dyed black over rust, so the rust comes back. Knits are dropped and laddered by hand. Bone is bound in copper and wire. There is one scent, and it smells of soil. Most pieces come in small numbered editions; two were made once. The Draped Mantle is cut only when someone writes, to their measure.
